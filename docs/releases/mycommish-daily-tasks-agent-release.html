Daily Tasks

From My commissions or My files, open Daily Tasks. You’ll see today’s due count, remaining, and completed — then a clean table for Day 1 and Day 5 follow-ups.

Who appears today: files enrolled 1 or 5 calendar days ago (US Pacific). If that day is a weekend or US federal holiday, the file rolls forward to the next business day. Dropped files are excluded.

How to use it

  1. Sign in to mycommish.app and click Daily Tasks.
  2. Switch between the Day 1 and Day 5 tabs.
  3. Call the Home phone on each row (click the number).
  4. Mark Email, SMS, and Call when each is done (all three required).

Columns on each file

  • Client name + External ID (AMOD)
  • Home phone
  • Enrolled debt · Enrolled date
  • 1st payment (scheduled) · 1st cleared (if they paid)
  • Pay freq (Monthly, Bi-Weekly, etc.)
  • Status — including Pending Affiliate Cancellation when applicable

Also worth knowing

Weekends & holidays

If day 1 or day 5 lands on Sat/Sun or a US federal holiday, the file shows on the next business day.

Pending cancellation

Still appears if not dropped yet. Status shows Pending Affiliate Cancellation so you can see risk early.

Checklist saved

Email / SMS / Call checks save to your login. Progress shows 0/3 → Done. Refresh keeps your marks.

Your book only

Scoped to your CRM Sales Rep alias(es). Ask an admin if names aren’t mapped.

Unchanged: Commissions, tiers, and clawback math are the same. Daily Tasks is about follow-up visibility — not payout calculation. Questions? Ask your manager or admin. · mycommish.app
